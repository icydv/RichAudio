## Narrow acoustic corrections

The original beacon now has an explicit finite-emitter near-field calibration, independent of audition trim. Only the one-source, open-road, original 600 Hz / 0.44 harmonic profile receives it. Other source profiles and future vehicle synthesis retain their existing policy.

For reference distance 1 and radius R=10 declared model metres, the pressure spreading distance is the inverse of `(1.5 - 0.5*(d/R)^2)/R` inside R. Outside R, the existing inverse-distance formula is unchanged bit for bit. Pressure and its first derivative meet continuously at R, and pressure has zero slope at the centre. This is an authored diagnostic-source calibration, not a measurement of GTA's physical scale. The source level remains 0.44, audition trim +12 dB, host gain 0.20 and pre-host ceiling 0.5. No block is clipped, repaired or normalized; unsafe blocks still fail closed.

At the original model distance sqrt(37)=6.08276, actual direct PCM is 1.93947 dB quieter. Its harmonic ratios and crest factor agree within 0.000001 dB. At 10 and 12 model metres, three-second original/corrected PCM hashes match exactly. At one metre, the correction is 16.50718 dB below the unsafe old point-source level. It still becomes stronger on approach; it no longer treats an almost coincident listener as a one-metre point-source reference.

The retained qualified two-surface/four-path evaluation reaches a maximum pre-host peak of 0.43258721, leaving 1.25793 dB below the unchanged 0.5 ceiling. It covers declared geometry scales 0.01–2, second-order arrivals, source/geometry replacements, reacquisition and expiry. The [calibration evidence](../evidence/acoustics-08-3-5-source-calibration.json) preserves all six peaks and failed smaller-radius evaluations. This is measured headroom for those bounded fixtures, not a universal bound for every allowed live reflected-path sum; the guard remains authoritative.

Calibration probes retained the failed radius-6 and radius-8 cases. They also demonstrated a distinct transition defect: a 20 ms old-path linear release overlapping a 10 ms new-path exponential attack can coherently reach 1.1534 times one arrival. Replacement source/reflection identities now use complementary linear attacks/releases. Existing independent arrivals, propagation delays, path validity and source history are not normalized or discarded. A valid near-zero delay also no longer waits forever for a current dry tap that the renderer itself writes before reading it.

An exact source-centre crossing exposed another causal defect: the smoothed approach rate could advance the read cursor past emitted PCM as propagation delay reached zero. The renderer now bounds cursor advance by available emitted-source time. This constrains the read slope, not the sample amplitude; it neither manufactures future audio nor resets history. The real-producer reproduction previously recorded 2,431 future reads affecting 1,288 frames and now records zero. Its bounded-rate interventions remain explicit diagnostics. A harmonic crossing regression retains the expected approximately 607 Hz approach and 593 Hz recession for a 600 Hz source and 4 model metres/second listener.

## Continuous listener presentation and timing

The live geometry-enabled route uses measured observation cadence and delivery age to adapt position playout within its existing 100 ms maximum. Delay converges only while the whole playable pose interval is stationary; it freezes during movement so clock slewing cannot create artificial velocity or false discontinuities. Position forecasting retains its existing 50 ms ceiling for observation gaps. Orientation uses the same retrospective observation bracket with no angular prediction. Quaternion reconstruction handles legitimate fast rotations without the singularity of blending opposite forward vectors. The renderer retains per-sample gain/pan evolution and smoothly changes retarded-time read rates instead of stepping them at each 512-frame control boundary.

Presentation uses one coherent, fresh listener pose after worker completion. It reprojects an existing retarded arrival's final world vertex to that pose for camera-relative direction; it does not invent a reflection, change source emission time, distance attenuation, Doppler solution, visibility or history. Session/generation/freshness protections and bounded invalid-path release remain authoritative. A changed camera is not treated as a moving sound source.

Where arrived observations bracket an output block, presentation supplies its known pan slope and the renderer advances it per sample. If only a prefix is covered, that prefix is used; unknown tail samples retain the existing fallback. Every crossed interval must remain valid and unpaused. A persistent residual follows changes in the path's reconstructed start without forcing a catch-up at each block boundary. Its 100 ms recovery time constant applies only to that error, not to the known trajectory or all camera motion, and is not a settling deadline. Each update stays between the previous actual pan and the current known target, preventing retained error from driving motion past that target. These are bounded panning coordinates; the audio samples and 0.5 amplitude guard are unchanged. No quaternion work, allocation, blocking or GPU wait is added to the native audio callback.

Native measurements also exposed a startup clock-alignment defect: the first observation could anchor QPC to audio-frame time before cold worker initialization and PCM prefill established their steady phase. Keeping original observation intervals fixed then preserved a startup-dependent clock-phase error. After at least 750 ms of genuinely valid, unpaused, exactly stationary position and orientation, the corrected route estimates one common offset from the last eight receipt mappings. It shifts all observation time labels and their anchor together, at most 100 ms, and seals that identity's calibration permanently. Original QPC ticks, intervals, wall-clock freshness, source PCM, history and audio-frame time stay unchanged. Any motion or unavailability during startup skips calibration. This avoids per-packet clock jitter and clock slewing during movement; it is not trajectory prediction. The applied/skipped reason, shift and receipt spread are bounded diagnostics.

The historical complete getter cadence and ring occupancy were not retained. SoundMap's roughly 16 Hz sampled trace must not be mistaken for the 20 Hz native listener stream. The historical observation-to-solver-publication measurement below already includes configured playout and observation bracketing; adding another 100 ms would double-count it.

## Failure diagnostics

The guard scans the entire bounded block before mutation. On failure it distinguishes nonfinite from over-ceiling output, retains the original frame/channel/first offending value, finite pre/post-trim maxima and numerical configuration. A lazy, whitelist-only context collector records source generations, distances and relative motion when available, output preferences, path counts/release/replacement state and changes since the previous block. Path-state counts are sampled after DSP at the failed block's end, not asserted to describe the exact offending sample. Context collection failure cannot mask the original guard condition. One bounded failure record is retained; no rejected audio payload is logged.
