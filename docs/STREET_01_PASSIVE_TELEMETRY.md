Dismount and a genuinely observed stopped engine can retain that vehicle; missing, dead or changed identity retires the diagnostic.

Codex found that successful rows previously omitted their full identity witnesses, and an unsuccessful native acquisition could leave only a broad retirement reason. Diagnostic ABI 2 now retains the original native/RPH witnesses for every comparison, the raw float32 bits from both getters, and one immutable native first-failure record. That record identifies the failed query/stage, original QPC interval, return status and available operands even when there was no successful sample. A later Stop cannot replace it. The comparer also latches the SHV handle across rows. Partial RPH witnesses mark which getters actually returned; partial native identities are explicitly incomplete. A default field in a failed capture is not an engine-off observation.

RPH can load a plugin assembly from a cache directory. The sidecar therefore resolves from the actual game executable's `Plugins` directory, independently of the assembly location. Manual `LoadPlugin` can also occur while the console pauses the game. Before arming, the diagnostic retains the chosen RPH Bati and waits at most ten seconds for observed unpaused/pause-menu-inactive readiness. It must remain the current local driver vehicle. Offline Story loss, unload, identity loss or expiration stops this phase; becoming ready after the original deadline cannot arm the sampler.

The native query remains the [pinned Enhanced declaration](https://github.com/alloc8or/gta5-nativedb-data/blob/424fb51b089049a9fbcebcc641500b1d44d255b4/natives_gen9.json#L112332), using the previously reviewed [SHV public ABI](https://github.com/scripthookvdotnet/scripthookvdotnet/blob/3a0b4786ea8cff050fbccf7993a2cfedc8d4a64f/source/loader/sdk/inc/main.h). Both peers retain their original QPC intervals and frequency. Lossless hexadecimal session, address and query-hash witnesses supplement JSON's numeric fields. The existing diagnostic rejects replay, a foreign session, inconsistent order, evidence older than 250 ms and peer skew above 50 ms. Its description call must return inside its original managed bracket within four milliseconds. These are diagnostic validity gates, not audio-latency measurements. Driver attachment is included; camera pose, rider-view attachment and airflow qualification are absent.

The native sampler is capped at 20 Hz. The managed loop sleeps for ten milliseconds and can poll approximately 100 times per second, making two RPH captures per poll even when there is no new native row. It has a hard `100 × maximumSeconds + 1` poll limit, at most 12,001 polls for this proposal. Pre-arm readiness has a separate 1,001-poll cap. Acquisition and the final fresh pre-arm capture add three captures beyond those readiness polls. The payload records actual polls, capture attempts and running/ratio getter attempts separately; unavailable acquisition counts are labelled. In one authored retained-lifetime control, 28 managed polls produced 60 RPH captures. That is source evidence, not a measured in-game cost. Getter/fiber CPU costs and live scheduling remain unqualified.

## Proposed single session

The prepared transaction changes exactly four targets: it creates the diagnostic ASI, RPH diagnostic DLL and sidecar, and temporarily replaces the retained `startup.rphs` command with an empty file. The normal main DLL and its settings remain intact and unloaded; the RPH INI is unchanged. The diagnostic ASI may register its dormant script at launch. Its RPH entry must be loaded manually only after I am already the driver of the selected Bati in offline Story Mode. The existing working launcher and verified game working directory must be used once, with no alternate launcher or automatic retry. OpenIV quarantine stays intact. Fresh preflight must reject changed identities or unexpected native modules before dispatch.

The independent watcher permits 600 seconds for startup/Story/Bati admission, then a conservative 133-second window measured from the manual load-intent witness: ten seconds of pre-arm readiness, 120 seconds of native observation and three seconds of writer completion. Normal exit has at most 180 seconds, within the 913-second overall bound. The load-intent witness is not proof that the plugin loaded. Pending or unobserved launch closure keeps restoration withheld.

The following phases fit the existing 120-second armed window. Their times are approximate offsets from the first accepted native observation; the raw QPC evidence determines actual boundaries. My actions are ordinary gameplay inputs, with no diagnostic engine-state or vehicle-control calls.

| Phase | Planned action and required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Acquisition / first 15 s | I remain stationary in the Bati. Both independently acquired identities must match; the same pool lifetime/native handle, raw values, running flags and fresh intervals must be retained. |
| Approximately 15–35 s | I rev gently while stationary. Each peer's raw operating values must show a plausible response without clipping, rescaling or substituted samples. |
| Approximately 35–55 s | I move normally, then stop safely. Identity and freshness must continue; operating response is assessed separately for each interface. |
| Approximately 55–80 s | I dismount and remain near the same vehicle. The log must distinguish driver detachment from entity retirement and preserve the original vehicle where valid. Dismount is not assumed to leave the engine running. |
| Approximately 80–110 s | Any agreed ordinary-gameplay running/off transition is observed directly. If it does not occur, that phase remains unobserved; no stopped state is fabricated to complete it. |
| End of window | The existing bound or an explicit stop retires the sampler. Genuine earlier source loss is classified from its first failure. There is no destructive action or silent reacquisition to manufacture a retirement result. |

The live supervisor must preserve the phase/action notes and actual timing alongside the telemetry. A phase is not proved merely because its planned time passed. Each phase needs its own identity, value validity/change, freshness, lifetime and running-versus-loss assessment. The experiment may stop at the first structural failure and leave later phases unobserved.

## Logging, shutdown and verdict

This proposal records private telemetry, not microphone, system audio or video. The sampler holds one latest observation plus one immutable first failure. The RPH side retains at most 2,400 rows; the serialized result has an eight-MiB byte ceiling. Nonfinite values retain their bits and a text representation. Exceeding the bound fails publication instead of dropping records or fields.

After Stop, the background owner observes the native script-loop-exit flag for at most 1,000 ms and retains the final native terminal record. Unknown closure retains its ordinary module reference. An armed module is process-pinned: an observed loop flag is not an external SHV callback join or safe hot-unload proof. I must exit the whole game and confirm owned process closure before the transaction restores, removes or replaces any diagnostic file.

The writer publishes new files atomically and never overwrites an existing result. `diagnostic.json` means a payload was prepared. It becomes usable evidence only with the same-session `writer-completion.json` whose SHA-256, byte count and row count match that exact payload, no writer-failure receipt, and the session-bound Main completion outcome. A writer failure has its own bounded receipt where the output location remains writable. Missing, partial or conflicting output cannot pass; a loop-exit observation does not make a failed writer complete.

The RPH Main fiber now yields until useful writer work completes or fails, with a separate three-second original-QPC deadline. Pause ticking is explicitly enabled. The background publisher checks that same budget; a delayed queued writer cannot publish a successful result after Main's expiration. The Main outcome is printed with its session, and an unknown/failure outcome disqualifies publication even if a file exists. This is a new diagnostic-only writer bound, not an audio deadline or an external ThreadPool-callback join. Forced plugin/AppDomain termination or whole-process exit can still prevent journalling; absence of completed evidence is a failed/unresolved result, not successful qualification.

The first observed failing boundary must be reported as binding, acquisition, getter, identity, timing, publication or shutdown, with its retained operands. Native and RPH observations are asynchronous; the first reported boundary is not necessarily the earliest event in the game. An invalid operating value can disqualify one interface without inventing a structural failure in the other. Steady running-state agreement and a genuine transition are assessed independently from tracking loss.

A useful result names the usable interface, accepted gameplay phases and supported retained lifetime—or the exact first boundary that prevented that conclusion. It does not enable that interface in normal audio telemetry. The normal RPH feed, selective native takeover/restoration, unexplained historical cold NPC discrepancy, complete world activation and failed processing deadlines remain separate open gates. Passing this passive experiment cannot establish audible STREET-01 readiness.
