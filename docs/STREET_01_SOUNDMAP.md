# STREET-01 SoundMap worksets

My SoundMap remains connected to the acoustic scene and selected paths used by the renderer. The installed static-world path can now publish up to 4,096 finite supports within an 8 MiB geometry packet. Ordinary fixtures retain the compact 128-surface / 512 KiB geometry limit, and path frames retain their 256 KiB bound. A missing prepared packet for a larger installed workset produces an explicit `prepared-geometry-required` omission, without rebuilding thousands of surfaces on the audio producer.

The persistent installed-scene worker prepares the geometry JSON from the same immutable scene that it encodes for the renderer. The producer checks the geometry key, tile observation key, catalogue, request, binding epoch, scene-packet digest and payload integrity before registering it. Small dynamic stream/session/generation headers are added at publication. The network worker receives transferred bytes and materialises the full viewer geometry there. Renderer controls and source PCM are unaffected; no audio buffer or future observation is added.

The existing transport still permits one request in flight and drops intervening visual updates. It does not wait for the service from an audio callback. The service retains at most 32 MiB of serialized geometry and the browser retains at most 16 MiB, while preserving their existing generation-count ceilings. These are serialized-byte bounds, not measurements of JavaScript heap usage. Oversized packets are rejected with a diagnostic, and a rejected generation is not reconstructed at every display update.

Codex exercised the telemetry, actual network worker, local service and browser model with an original 4,096-support fixture. The viewer retained exactly the renderer's finite footprints, selected path identities, delays and gains. The fixture also checked catalogue/request/epoch/integrity mismatches, omission without preparation, cache eviction, rejection above 8 MiB, dropped updates and resend after the encoded master had already been transferred. No installed resource, GTA launch, audio endpoint or recording was used for this test.

The first [private receipt](../.local/street01/soundmap-tests/d336f521-3e03-4647-bf28-cafe035f0a6d/validation.json) retained 17.7188 ms geometry construction and 14.1195 ms producer-side structured cloning. Those costs exceeded the audio block deadline and are not accepted performance evidence. After preparation moved to the existing scene worker, the [prepared receipt](../.local/street01/soundmap-tests/cb11a974-065f-45d1-abf8-885452f5f943/prepared-validation.json) retained a 2,269,026-byte body, 20.2912 ms preparation outside the producer, 1.2765 ms registration, 0.0123 ms geometry publication, 0.3927 ms client offer and 81.0607 ms complete asynchronous transfer. The components describe different stages; they must not be presented as a total audio latency measurement.

This verifies bounded transport of the same acoustic state. It does not establish genuine installed-world coverage, physical material calibration, live reflection quality or complete audio deadlines. Scene admission and complete worker costs still require their own measured acceptance, including every remaining miss.
