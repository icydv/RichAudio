# Vehicle audio engine increment 01

I want RichAudio to own every vehicle sound eventually. The existing 49-entry component registry defines the target scope. This increment adds authored transmission and brake instruments to the common component lifecycle, clock, position, and propagation renderer. Gear mesh uses shaft speed times tooth count, with a bounded load envelope and continuous phase. Brake friction uses rotor sliding speed, pad force, and friction as excitation for a provisional structural mode and noise model. Neither model derives its inputs from an unqualified GTA getter. The current product still has additive Bati engine, exhaust, and airflow. The authored component and collision fixtures are a separate level of evidence.

The normal product path is the RPH capture, gameplay pipe, Windows host, Node StockGameplay producer, acoustic solver, RA07 pipe, and host PCM ring. Installed-scene admission keeps its source proof and original lease. In the solver, each finite path measures its legs once and rejects impossible one-sided bounces before CPU visibility or GPU dispatch. Its final status, material decisions and sample clock are preserved. The exact 48 kHz / 512-frame contract is 10.667 ms per block. The candidate leaves the WASAPI path and buffer sizes as reviewed.

Codex used the actual engine and exhaust emission positions to prepare a retained scene with 1,445 surfaces. At an authored source position paired with a retained listener pose, the scene withheld the exhaust direct path when it found a known hit and unresolved coverage. The engine direct path remained. The same conditional-direct control without the scene admitted both paths. The three arms matched dry PCM and source-history hashes. All three WAVs were also byte-identical to the corresponding arms from the starting source. The retained reflection comparison withheld candidate paths because collision-layer activation was unresolved. The authored canyon and tunnel runs produced second-order paths.

The PLAYABLE-15 stem check reproduced the original to within 3.73e−9. Exhaust exceeded engine by 27.91 dB RMS and the cross-correlation was −0.0103. The evidence supports mix and source spectrum as the dominant causes in this trace. The audition raises engine relative to exhaust by 18.06 dB and applies an audition-only level match. The two files measure −34.57 LUFS. Welch power below 600 Hz falls from 96.83% to 93.57%. Both retain about 99.9% of stereo RMS when folded to mono. The in-game pressure policy retains its existing calibration.
