## Capture configuration and evidence

First-load profile: 20 Hz, eight selected sources, census inspection cap 128, queue 64, soft acquisition budget 2 ms, drivetrain reads enabled, capture initially disabled, output in `__USER_HOME__\Documents\RichAudio\.local\captures`. One background writer flushes every 20 records; no per-source files or disk writes from the sampling loop. The queue holds at most 64 records, about 3.2 s at 20 Hz, then drops new records and reports losses. This is a bounded record count, not a hard memory-byte ceiling. Enumeration/getters cannot be preempted by the soft budget.

An authored mock record serialized with the real JSONL serializer projects **22.66 MiB/min at eight sources**, versus **86.43 MiB/min at 32**. These are arithmetic projections, not game data or physical disk benchmarks; values, lifecycle events and strings change size. See [size evidence](../evidence/phase2.1/logging-size-projection.json). Begin with 10 s, then 30–120 s marked captures. Keep at least 2 GiB free, inspect growth after each run and stop manually if unexpected. There is no implemented automatic duration/quota/rotation feature. A later 32-source distance survey requires its own hashed configuration review after timing headroom is measured.

Commands: `RichAudioStatus`, `RichAudioMark urban_TestA`, `RichAudioStart`, `RichAudioStop`; graceful unload using RPH help. Console pause/resume can legitimately segment a run; analyse a console-free uninterrupted interval for cadence. Preserve raw JSONL and RPH logs locally; match scene labels to operator video/timing notes. Never infer that a well-formed capture passed accuracy or frame-time tests.

## A–E acceptance protocol (all live results pending)

First pass a 60 s RPH-only baseline and plugin load/status/10 s capture smoke test. Require command registration, no exceptions, nonempty sample records, a valid header/footer after graceful unload, and no forbidden writes. A load/API binding failure stops the experiment.

Common numerical gates for uninterrupted selected-target captures: strictly increasing monotonic sample timestamps, no duplicate TrackingId within a batch, no NaN/infinite values marked observed/estimated, no unexplained invalid entity reuse, no JSON/schema errors, and successful graceful writer drain within the implemented 2 s unload allowance. Record every missing field's status/reason. Require observed player model/position/velocity/engine state in at least 99% of selected, continuously valid player observations; player RPM/gear availability >=95% when drivetrain reads are applicable. Availability does not alone establish physical accuracy. Derived acceleration/distance/camera orientation remain estimated; load and physical crankshaft RPM remain unavailable without calibration.

Cadence gate: target 20 Hz independently scheduled by monotonic deadlines, observed rate 19–21 Hz, at least 95% of eligible scheduled instants represented within 100 ms of their deadline, p95 schedule delay <=50 ms, no unexplained sequence gaps or queue drops. Exclude and label explicit pauses/capture-disabled intervals; retain stalls and missed-deadline counters. Use raw deadline timestamps plus cumulative missed counts to calculate the 100 ms percentage; the current analyzer's summary alone does not compute it. Low render/scheduler rates can prevent this gate; do not fix it using unsafe timer-thread game calls.

| Test | Controlled procedure | Additional acceptance/evidence |
|---|---|---|
| A | Stationary player car: engine off 30 s, then idle 60 s, fixed camera | When stationary, median speed <0.5 m/s and position drift <1 m over a stable 10 s window; compare visible rolling/physics before judging. Engine transitions match the video within 250 ms; do not infer engine-off from zero ratio. Confirm stable identity/model. |
| B | Motorcycle: three acceleration, gear-change and deceleration cycles; deliberate camera change separately | At least three visible gear transitions agree with observed index changes within 250 ms. Ratio remains in its documented range or explicitly unavailable. Compare trend against acceleration/overrun; do not require a calibrated RPM value or infer load from throttle. Derivatives do not bridge a camera cut/long tracking gap. |
| C | Nearby urban traffic, stationary listener, 120 s | Observe at least three distinct driven non-player vehicles if naturally available; one identity per live token/generation and no player double count. Report engine/RPM/gear availability and unexplained frozen/zero values per vehicle. A sparse natural scene is inconclusive, not a forced spawn. |
| D | Urban and rural/highway observation; 25/50/100/200/400+ m analysis bands | Report total exposed count, selected counts, omissions, maximum sampled distance and drivetrain availability by band. Empty/censored bands remain explicit. No promised radius or distant-vehicle accuracy threshold: this experiment establishes the limitation. A capped eight-source prefix cannot establish a global streaming radius. |
| E | Leave/return normally across streaming boundaries, then pause/resume and stop/restart capture | Confirm first/continued/unavailable/termination events against the scene. Inspect full token/index/counter/model changes; no duplicate active IDs, stale derivatives or implicit persistent identity across gaps. Require both disappearance and reappearance evidence, or mark test inconclusive. |

Compare values to visible behaviour; video is a qualitative reference, not precision ground truth. Confirm metre/basis assumptions with repeatable world landmarks before claiming distance accuracy. NPC fields may be unavailable or synthetic at range; report semantic disagreements instead of fabricating replacements. [NPC_STREAMING.md](NPC_STREAMING.md) supplies the full distance protocol; no live distances have been measured.

Performance: matched route/settings, three runs each of ordinary baseline, RPH-only, RichAudio loaded/capture-off and capture-on. Record the actual external frame-time measurement tool/version; no measurement tool was installed in this phase. Use an existing suitable monitor, or review a separate measurement plan before adding one. Require paired median/p95 frame-time increase <=5% and <=1 ms at p95, no reproducible new >100 ms stalls, capture-on incremental process CPU <=3 percentage points of whole-machine CPU, and no unexplained private-memory growth >50 MiB over 10 min. These are experimental gates, not measurements. Record clock, GPU/CPU load, traffic variance and baseline tool overhead; inconclusive variance requires repeat runs. Require acquisition p95 <=2 ms, processing p95 <=0.2 ms, with p99/max and over-budget counts retained. No crash/hang/writer error in the smoke test, A–E and then 10 min capture. Longer 30 min turnover tests follow only after those pass. No stability guarantee follows from finite tests.
