## Production correction

The normal runner binds its actual StockGameplay consumer to the persistent scene service. Cache publication and candidate validity no longer announce consumer readiness. The service records preparing, candidate, active, lost, stopping and stopped states; a candidate's explicit admission decision records whether it is suitable for first activation. The status shown by my existing sidebar follows actual consumer state and retains cache-preparation progress separately.

Before first activation or rearming after loss, the service requires at least three distinct completed warm candidates in the same bounded source/workload scope. It retains at most 16 completed outcomes and a separate 16 numeric known-admission costs. A slow authenticated admission still raises the bound if its candidate is later coalesced; it does not count as another completed training outcome. Cold component preparation, source refresh and predominantly new panel-template preparation do not masquerade as warm evidence. Incremental templates during smooth movement remain part of measured warm work. Candidate validation can warm immutable geometry and, when requested, SoundMap state without replacing the active scene, rendering PCM or advancing source/grain history.

The forecast includes the high-water dispatch-to-admission cost plus its observed range, complete admission-to-application waiting, a 10.6667 ms enforced ceiling for the whole synchronous consumer transaction, and measured observation/dispatch scheduling variation. Already known current-candidate latency and waiting raise that same bound before first activation. In-flight work that has exceeded its modeled completion, or whose predicted application would reach its own original expiry, cannot promise a successor. The same feasible forecast drives active renewal qualification and the sidebar's explicit at-risk stage. That ceiling preserves the existing 512-frame/48 kHz hard deadline; it is a conservative declared bound, not a measured worst-case execution guarantee or permission to consume the whole audio block. The separate 8 ms headroom target and complete render checks remain.

For first activation, remaining original validity must exceed successor availability/dispatch/worker wait, complete successor delivery/application and the measured scheduling margin. Concurrent waits overlap in that calculation. Pending age must also leave the successor able to meet its own unchanged deadline. If the evidence or reserve is insufficient, my product stays preparing and records why. A separate, distinct observation must create the next pose-bound scene. No old header is refreshed.

The model binds catalogue, epoch, generation, exact selected resource proof, spatial work class, density/capacity, preparation mode and consumer settings/backend. Significant observation delay and changed workloads require new evidence. Once geometry is active, a useful fresh matching replacement may extend its actual deadline while renewal is explicitly at risk; it is not discarded solely because an estimate changed. Actual expiry remains a sticky qualification failure, invokes direct fallback and cannot be repaired retroactively by later recovery.

The scheduler starts a useful pending request before the publication callback. Its ordinary minimum interval remains 500 ms. Deadline-directed urgency can bring it forward only within 250–500 ms, with a hard maximum of four dispatches per second, one worker and one latest pending request. It never rebuilds at audio-block frequency or waits synchronously for the worker in the audio callback. Source bytes, component ownership, one-shot continuation, adaptive radius, clipping, materials, omissions and reflection selection retain their existing contracts.

An active proof watch sends only bounded source-identity metadata over the existing worker channel. It checks the worker's already parsed current rows on normal manifest refresh, without extra archive reads or decoding. Withdrawal of an active resource's proof revokes that scene; unrelated whole-world validation progress does not. The consumer checks its actual active workset rather than treating the broader 128-unit safety tile as full prepared coverage.

Stop has one immutable monotonic boundary. Both production and qualification fence observation requests, dispatch, queue adoption and application at that boundary, before asynchronous cleanup waits. They first preserve any lease already expired at stop. Late results remain diagnostic evidence and cannot reactivate the consumer. An expiry during an asynchronous acoustic calculation is checked before renderer acceptance; direct fallback reuses that block's existing observations and source/grain history.

Coalescing or cancelling a pending candidate retains a bounded original-header discard receipt with no application timestamp. Its known queue cost stops at that boundary, its discarded ticket cannot later authorize mutation, and it adds neither an application nor a completed warm sample.

The qualification harness observes this production policy and requires actual activation plus at least ten renewals and a fresh qualified terminal scene. It preserves every post-activation gap and all fail-before-action/bankless guards. Its first-consumer artifact contains bounded scalar provenance and timing metadata, is published after handover and reports its own bytes/CPU/wall cost. Prepared geometry is never serialized into that artifact.
