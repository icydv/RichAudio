## Registration is a separate dependency

The [Cfx data-file source](https://github.com/citizenfx/fivem-docs/blob/03c259d81808ea80519429c1e70621b818d9460f/content/docs/game-references/data-files.md) distinguishes game, sound, curve, synth and speech metadata from wave-pack mounting. It describes resource-manifest entries and their mounters. This is evidence for FiveM's loading surface, not a public equivalent in my Enhanced single-player RPH stack. RichAudio does not install another loader or invoke a named mounter inferred from that table.

The retained [CodeWalker REL parser](https://github.com/dexyfex/CodeWalker/blob/485d56bec00262ed7fa472261cce7bbc6202b96e/CodeWalker.Core/GameFiles/FileTypes/RelFile.cs#L10262) exposes separate engine/exhaust pre/post-submix fields and submix references. Those serialized integers and hashes are useful for a future dependency audit. They do not demonstrate physical gain units, a valid mute value, runtime gain automation or a callable DSP insertion point. No proprietary profile or bank has been copied, edited or redistributed.

**Inference:** the credible profile route still requires a unique original metadata graph and an exact-stack registration/loading contract. The complete qualification chain is: register unique records without altering shared originals; resolve their transitive dependencies and effective precedence; assign the graph to the exact vehicle lifetime; measure component and same-model isolation; recover the actual prior state; and prove restoration under controller/plugin/host failure. Passing an earlier link cannot substitute for the later links. Reading `vehicles.meta` would establish a model default, not automatically the effective per-instance runtime state.

There is no verified expiry/lease backend in the reviewed surface that can undo a persistent native mutation after the component that issued it dies. An external audio host, `finally` callback or ordinary game exit does not independently prove seamless fail-open restoration. No engine shutdown, whole-game/session mute or guessed empty profile is substituted.

## Consequence for this integration increment

Read-only horn, engine-state, gear and contact observations can improve identity, occurrence-interval and physical-evidence contracts while GTA continues to own their sounds. Their existence does not establish suppression. An observed transition is not permission to layer a duplicate RichAudio horn, impact or other direct component over the corresponding native sound. Scope loss and an observed engine-off transition also remain different events.

The geometry/resource and observation adapters may therefore advance offline while the production event bus and native ownership remain disabled. Authored original PCM and deterministic replay establish software behavior only. A future observation-only runtime proposal must require no native mutation or replacement audio; a later ownership proposal additionally needs a verified mechanism, known original state and independent recovery. I do not have a credible executable native-takeover candidate to approve at this stage.
