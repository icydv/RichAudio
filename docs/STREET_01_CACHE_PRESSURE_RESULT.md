## Implementation

Codex replaced duplicated whole-query geometry arrays with bounded immutable source components and lazily boxed triangle/panel templates. Current selected chunk bytes are still read and SHA-256 verified. The combined component/descriptor retention ceiling remains 64 MiB, with at most eight metadata-only descriptors; boxed geometry retains its previous conservative charge. Exact coordinates, materials, provenance and current clipping/reflection rules remain. [Architecture, accounting and invalidation](STREET_01_COMPONENT_CACHE.md) describes the ownership and source-binding contracts.

Adaptive selection now requires a real capacity witness before discarding a wide pass, then materializes only the final tile/index/scene. It preserves the original radius policy and conservative omissions. Static preparation may warm immutable components after an observation expires, while that expired observation remains unpublished. Every accepted scene retains its original request, generation and deadline.

A one-shot continuation lets an unexpired original request consume its already completed selection in the same synchronous turn. It binds that request's complete inputs and source revisions, then is destroyed before materialization. Expired or superseded work cannot turn it into a later observation. This removes the duplicate selection and current-byte reads exposed by the first candidate; subsequent observations still perform their own current verification.
