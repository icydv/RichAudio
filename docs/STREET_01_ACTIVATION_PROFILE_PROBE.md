## Private Bati profile capability

Codex traced ten Bati routes for the initial continuous engine and exhaust candidate. They include player granular acceleration and idle synth, conventional engine and exhaust idle, low and high loops, and the revs-off sound. The graph comparison uses a private vehicle record, two private controller records, and ten private sound records. Shared acceleration references stay on the existing records for NPCs. Horns, collisions, transmission, start/stop and limiter effects stay on their original routes. The retained granular engine and exhaust each still reference a synth and preset. Their contribution and other runtime scheduling must be measured before I can claim full continuous suppression.

The standalone probe prepares two private DAT151 vehicle records from a pinned complete base record. Each has a unique name and one changed horn sound-list link. The preparation tool keeps those list hashes separate from the expected values of the default horn getter. It writes the REL and the proposed AUDIO_GAMEDATA fragment privately. The fragment must be mounted by an approved loader path before it can prove registration. The authored smoke test established file creation and field distinction. [Default-horn getter declaration](https://github.com/citizenfx/natives/blob/master/AUDIO/GetVehicleDefaultHorn.md).

The read-only console command observes the selected vehicle's default horn hash and reports the loaded RPH assembly version. This can establish distinct readbacks for two known donor sound-list links without requesting playback. The separate marker command requires a mounted pair with independently pinned expected hashes. It creates two disposable Batis at the approved empty location, leaves the neighbour alone, and observes A, B, then A on the target. The probe validates the full pool handle, counter, address and model before a write. Both target and neighbour must remain empty and the engine must be off. Cleanup retires the owned lifetimes on completion and ordinary managed failure; a recycled or occupied vehicle is excluded from writes. The authored RPH fixture verifies the A/B/A sequence, loss of the readback, normal cleanup and recycled-identity refusal.

A positive A/B/A result would establish resolution, per-vehicle assignment and restoration of a known private marker. It would leave the exact effective profile of an arbitrary existing Bati unresolved, since different profiles can share a horn. Controller loss has no independent recovery mechanism in this probe. Production takeover remains disabled until an exact effective-state readback or equivalent reversible mechanism and a recovery path outside the failed controller are demonstrated. My next approval can cover the read-only camera and horn observations together; mounting, spawning, assigning profiles, playback and capture have separate scopes.
