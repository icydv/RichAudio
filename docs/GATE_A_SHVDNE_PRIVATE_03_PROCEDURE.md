## Result criteria

- **PASS — functional private contribution:** complete original marker in A after the single correctly targeted request; both independent baselines and usable post-B control are marker-free; video/journal prove intended horn origin and lifetimes; no observed unintended shared change; normal observed bike behavior; scope, deadlines and normal cleanup/restoration pass. This supports one functional private contribution and private-profile selection, not full registration coverage or ownership.
- **FAIL — demonstrated condition failure:** identify the exact failed prerequisite, authenticated isolation violation, incompatible required mechanism, abnormal behavior, deadline or cleanup condition. Verified marker from B's own horn is an isolation failure. A marker heard near B without attributable origin can instead be capture contamination and remains ambiguous.
- **INCONCLUSIVE — insufficient attribution:** absent/partial/unusable marker, clipping, silence, missing event/identity evidence, external contamination, A residue, inadequate recording or ambiguous selection. Marker absence alone does not establish loader incompatibility or inability to register the profile. A signal-level positive cannot override failed scope/lifecycle evidence.

No native engine/exhaust suppression, silent profile, production switching/takeover, crash/forced-unload test, custom sound-mod change, additional RAGE hook/patch or acoustic-engine change is included. The eventual suppression, safe restoration and custom-mod requirements remain unchanged; shared acoustics and future non-vehicle adapters remain separate. The authorization is consumed by this one attempt regardless of outcome. No repeat or later stage follows automatically.
