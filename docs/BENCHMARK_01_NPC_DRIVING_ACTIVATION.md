## One bounded activation policy

I retain setup inside the single Start intent. After successful setup the console instructs me to close F4. A pure readiness check requires known closed console, known unpaused state and two consecutive usable observations with game time increasing by at most 1000 milliseconds. Unknown/frozen/backward clock observations do not qualify; unsigned rollover is handled. No collision query is added.

There is a **ten-second monotonic readiness deadline** after Start, then the existing **five-second settling deadline** and 0.3-second continuous stationary requirement. This is a bounded preparation gate, not a new stall threshold. If gameplay readiness disappears during settling, its stationary evidence resets without extending the settling deadline. After activation, loss of readiness aborts rather than silently freezing or restarting the watchdog. The existing pause guard still terminates immediately on a reported pause; a paused reading may therefore be absent, with the footer preserving the guard reason. The overall controller deadline is checked even before first task issuance. No wait is indefinite and no task is reissued merely because the bike stays stationary.

Clock advancement and closed console are necessary admission observations, not proof of AI simulation. Generic task status is copied without setting TaskFailed from an unsupported interpretation. UNKNOWN, NoTask, interrupted-looking and active-looking labels remain distinct raw findings; zero movement is independently measured. The original one task per lookahead checkpoint policy is unchanged.
